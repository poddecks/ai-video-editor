# My Editing Rules

> Plain-English rules Claude follows on EVERY edit.
> When you give a note that should stick, say "add this to my rules" and Claude adds it here.

## Always
- Every video gets captions.
- Put hook text on screen in the first second.
- Keep the best take when I repeat a line, and cut the earlier attempts.
- Keep captions and graphics inside the safe zone (away from the bottom ~350px and the right ~150px, where the app buttons sit).

## Never
- Never cut in the middle of a word.
- Never leave a pause longer than a beat.
- Never change what I said in captions except to fix spelling.

## Callouts
- Add 1 to 3 callouts per video on the key numbers, steps, or punchlines.
